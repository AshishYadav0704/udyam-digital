Team Shankhnad, MMMUT Gorakhpur. Airtel SYNC 2026, Enterprise Track A. November 2026. Covers the prototype in this repository.
Prototype on the competition's synthetic data. Accel Business is the case study's fictional operator. This note describes how the system is designed and what is implemented; it is not legal advice and not a claim of compliance or certification. Counsel must review it before launch.

4. Telecom KYC and decisions about people

  • DoT business connections: each end user of a business mobile line completes their own KYC before the SIM is activated; the tracker shows this step. The prototype does not perform end-user KYC.
  • No automatic rejection: unclear or conflicting documents go to a KYC reviewer, who records a reason. OCR and models never approve or reject alone.
  • The lead score only orders call queues; it never changes price, eligibility or KYC. Capacity is reserved for non-GST micro businesses the model ranks low.
