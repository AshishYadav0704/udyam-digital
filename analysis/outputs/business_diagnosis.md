## Headline
- 5,000 leads -> 368 activations = **7.36%** lead-to-activation.
- Blended CAC = total acquisition spend Rs 5,539,640 / 368 activations = **Rs 15,053**.
- Activated customers: mean bundle Rs 12,351/month (median Rs 6,900), 17.6 days to activate,
  3.0 status-chasing calls, 6-month churn 15.2%, NPS index -25.
- Steepest stage drop: **Needs assessed -> Quote accepted (44%)**. Of the 1,113 leads lost there,
  621 cite price and 385 cite a slow or confusing quote.
- 827 leads were lost because no needs-discovery visit happened (owner had no time / visit not scheduled).

## Registration effect
- With GSTIN: 9.6% activation; KYC completion after quote 81%.
- Without GSTIN: 2.0% activation; KYC completion after quote **25%** - KYC is where non-GST businesses fail.

## Retention signals (correlational, activated customers only)
- Status calls vs NPS / churn: 1 call -> NPS 7.5, churn 6%; 5 calls -> NPS 6.0, churn 28%.
- Activation within 15 days -> mean NPS 7.3; over 21 days -> 6.0.
- Speed of first contact shows no relationship with activation (0-4 days: 9.8%-12.1%).

## Pricing evidence
- Realised bundle prices sit at 48% of the catalogue min-max range on average; a published midpoint
  price would have produced 100.2% of the realised MRR (revenue-neutral transparency).
- Price-related drops rise from 26% to 36% as the asked-for bundle grows relative to current telecom spend.
